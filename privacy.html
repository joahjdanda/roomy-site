Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. Applies to the Roomy app for iPhone and this website.

In short: Roomy doesn't collect any data about you. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, and the app has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no account.

What the app accesses, and why

With your permission, Roomy reads the photos and videos in your library so it can find duplicates, similar shots, burst leftovers, blurry or accidental shots, screenshots, screen recordings, large and duplicate videos, and Live Photos, and to show you photos taken on today's date in earlier years (On This Day). You can give it access to all of your photos or only some; you can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Roomy only changes your library when you ask it to:

  • Removing photos or videos you choose. iPhone asks you to confirm, and removed items stay in your Recently Deleted album for 30 days.
  • Saving new copies when you choose to compress or trim a video or turn a Live Photo into a still. Your original stays until you choose to remove it.

Faces in your photos. When choosing which photo in a group of similar shots to suggest as the best, Roomy uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to check whether faces look sharp and whether anyone's eyes are closed. It doesn't recognize or identify people, doesn't store anything about faces, and nothing about faces leaves your iPhone.

All of this analysis happens on the device. Nothing about your photos (not the pictures, their contents, locations or dates) is sent to us or to anyone else.

Contacts. Only if you open Contacts in Roomy and allow access, Roomy reads your contacts to find cards with the same name that share a phone number or email, and cards with no phone or email. It changes them only when you tap Merge or Delete, and first saves a full backup (.vcf file) inside the app so you can undo for 30 days. Roomy doesn't read contact notes and never touches your calendar. Contacts never leave your iPhone unless you share a backup file yourself.

Face ID or passcode. The Private Vault opens with Face ID or your passcode. iPhone only tells Roomy whether it worked; Roomy never sees your face data.

What's stored, and where

To save your progress, Roomy keeps its scan results, your keep/remove choices, small photo fingerprints used to spot duplicates, the list of photos you asked it never to suggest again, a history of what it removed (date, number of items, size), your Swipe Clean and On This Day progress, and a few counters (such as how many free removals you've used and your On This Day streak) inside the app on your iPhone. This never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Photos you move into the Private Vault are stored inside Roomy with iPhone's strongest file protection (encrypted whenever your iPhone is locked). They are never uploaded. Deleting Roomy deletes them, so save anything important back to Photos first.

If you add the Roomy widget, the app saves a small summary for it on your iPhone (space to clean, when you last scanned and how many On This Day photos there are today). The widget never opens your photo library.

Roomy also reads how much free space your iPhone has, to show it to you and to make sure there's room before saving a new copy.

Purchases

Subscriptions (and the lifetime unlock some people bought earlier) are bought and managed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs. It doesn't connect to any server of ours. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

If you email us

If you choose to contact support, your email (and anything you include in it) reaches us through your own email app. We use it only to answer you, and we delete support emails when they're no longer needed to help you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, and its fonts and images are served from this site. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Roomy doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If Roomy ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in the App Store before it happens.
