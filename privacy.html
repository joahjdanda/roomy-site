Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Applies to the Roomy app for iPhone and this website.

In short: Roomy doesn't collect any data about you. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, and the app has no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no account.

What the app accesses, and why

With your permission, Roomy reads the photos and videos in your library so it can find duplicates, similar shots, burst leftovers, blurry or accidental shots, screenshots, screen recordings, large videos and Live Photos. You can give it access to all of your photos or only some; you can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Roomy only changes your library when you ask it to:

  • Removing photos or videos you choose. iPhone asks you to confirm, and removed items stay in your Recently Deleted album for 30 days.
  • Saving new copies when you choose to compress a video or turn a Live Photo into a still. Your original stays until you choose to remove it.

All of this analysis happens on the device. Nothing about your photos (not the pictures, their contents, locations or dates) is sent to us or to anyone else.

What's stored, and where

To save your progress, Roomy keeps its scan results, your keep/remove choices, small photo fingerprints used to spot duplicates, and a few counters (such as how many free removals you've used) inside the app on your iPhone. This never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Roomy also reads how much free space your iPhone has, to show it to you and to make sure there's room before saving a new copy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are bought and managed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs. It doesn't connect to any server of ours. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

If you email us

If you choose to contact support, your email (and anything you include in it) reaches us through your own email app. We use it only to answer you, and we delete support emails when they're no longer needed to help you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, and its fonts and images are served from this site. GitHub, as the host, may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Roomy doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If Roomy ever starts collecting data, we'll say so here and in the App Store before it happens.
