Plans and purchases

How do I cancel my subscription?

In Roomy, open Settings (the gear on the Home screen) and tap Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Roomy and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge. Roomy stays unlocked until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Roomy, then tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen or in Roomy's Settings. Weekly and yearly subscriptions, and a lifetime unlock bought earlier, are all restored this way.

What's free, and what does unlocking add?

Free to try: your first 50 removals are free, then 15 free removals every day. Merging contacts and the Private Vault are part of Roomy Pro. Roomy Pro gives unlimited removals: Weekly at $7.99 per week (new subscribers get a 3-day free trial first) or Yearly at $39.99 per year. Both renew automatically until you cancel. If you cancel during the free trial, you aren't charged.

I was charged but Roomy still says locked.

Tap Restore purchase. If you used Ask to Buy, the purchase unlocks once it's approved. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your photos

I removed something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. Open the Photos app, go to Collections › Recently Deleted, select the items and tap Recover.

Why didn't my free space go up after removing photos?

Removed photos and videos wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use space until then. To get the space back sooner, open Photos › Collections › Recently Deleted, tap Select, then Delete All.

Does Roomy upload my photos?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Roomy only sees some of my photos.

You may have given Roomy access to only some photos. To change it, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Roomy and choose Full Access. Photos stored only in iCloud need to be downloaded to the iPhone before Roomy can compare or compress them.

Is compressing a video safe?

Roomy saves the smaller copy first, with the original's date, location and favorite, and checks that it plays. Only then can you choose to remove the original. If the copy wouldn't be meaningfully smaller, Roomy doesn't keep it.

What Roomy can't do

iOS doesn't let any app clear other apps' data, "junk files", system storage or memory, so Roomy doesn't claim to. It only removes, compresses or converts the photos and videos you choose. For other ways to free space, Roomy's More space tips shows where to find iPhone's own settings, such as Offload Unused Apps and keeping messages for 1 year.
