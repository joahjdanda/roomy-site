What Roomy finds

Duplicates

Copies of the same photo, even re-saved or resized ones. Roomy suggests the best copy to keep.

Similar photos and bursts

Shots taken seconds apart, and the burst frames you didn't pick.

Blurry and accidental shots

Very blurry, too dark or nearly blank photos, shown as suggestions only.

Screenshots and big videos

Old screenshots, screen recordings and your largest videos, biggest first.

Save space without losing anything

Compress videos

Re-save big videos in the space-saving HEVC format. You see the new size first, and your original stays until you choose.

Live Photos to stills

Keep the photo, drop the moving part. The still is saved and checked first.

You stay in control

Every group shows the photo being kept next to the ones to remove. iPhone asks you to confirm every removal, and removed items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Everything happens on your iPhone: your photos are never uploaded, and there are no ads, no tracking and no account.

Pricing

Your first 100 removals are free. Then choose Roomy Pro Yearly ($19.99 per year, renews automatically, cancel anytime) or Lifetime ($39.99, one-time purchase). No weekly or monthly plans and no free trial that turns into a charge.
